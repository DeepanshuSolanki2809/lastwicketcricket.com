ABOUT THE STUDIO

Not a channel. A cricket studio.

LAST Wicket Cricket started as one fan playing Cricket07. It's still run by one person but it's grown into an independent cricket media and entertainment project: videos, stories, and now, games.

Everything here gets built the way a good spell gets bowled, one over at a time, with intent. No investors, no studio behind it. Just someone who thinks cricket deserves a better corner of the internet.
